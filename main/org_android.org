There are a number of different programs for viewing and editing [[id:169b9c5f-df34-46ab-b64f-8ee98946ee69][Orgmode]] files on [[id:2c46e54a-d704-4e7e-bca3-d8c3e042ab43][Android]].

* Syncing Files

The easiest way to keep files in sync is using [[id:be393585-6c62-4ce9-9fba-15e4d1c472cb][SyncThing]] to keep your main repository on a computer on your home or work
network in sync with files on your phone.

* Clients

** [[https://www.zettelnotes.com/][Zettel Notes]]

A closed-source application (claims to be open-source but can find the repository). Pretty full-featured.

** [[https://orgro.org/][Orgro]]

Simple and effective browser, can toggle rendering of Org markup.

** [[https://github.com/rrajath/grove][grove]]

"/A task management and note-taking app for Android that is compatible with org-mode/", not tried this yet.

** [[https://gitlab.com/JohnRenz/emborg][emborg]]

For viewing and managing of ToDo tasks across multiple files.

** [[https://github.com/orgzly-revived/orgzly-android-revived][Orgzly Revived]]

The (almost!) original Org client for Android.
