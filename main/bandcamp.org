[[https://bandcamp.com][Bandcamp]] is a [[id:60363eb0-6272-4c26-8f2e-d7c237bd6609][music]] streaming and sales service. I've used it for a number of years and like its business model, its
pretty generous to artists (particularly on [[https://isitbandcampfriday.com/][Bandcamp Friday]]).

* Software

There are various [[id:604b59c7-4ca0-44a7-bbec-7f1486bc5bd8][Music Software]] packages available for Bandcamp.

** bandcampsync

[[https://github.com/meeb/bandcampsync/][meeb/bandcampsync: Downloads your Bandcamp purchases automatically]] I am yet to try it though as I typically reorganise
and rename my downloads as I don't like the ~## Artist name - Album name - track name.flac~ that Bandcamp uses (I prefer
structure ~music/albums/artist/album/## track name.flac~).

** bandcamper

[[https://github.com/yyyyyyyan/bandcamper][yyyyyyyan/bandcamper: Bandcamp downloader]] is another tool for downloading music from bandcamp.
