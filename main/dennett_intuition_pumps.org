** By Parody of Reasoning

+ Look for contradictions in assertions/conjectures when taken to the extreme.
+ If something is obviously flawed why do some people spend so much time trying to demolish the idea?
+ aside: materialism asserts that the /mind/ is the brain, whilst dualists assert that it is a separate entity that
interacts with the physical brain.
** Rapoports Law
+ Always point out obvious flaws.
+ Expose hidden contradictions,  but be wary of straying into nitpicking/parody.
+ aside: Anatol Rapoport devised the tit-for-tat strategy in the Prisoner's dilemma!
+ Successful critical commentary consists of...
  1. Express your point clearly (such that your opposition says "/Thanks, I'd never thought of it like that/")
  2. List points of agreement.
  3. Mention what you have learned from others points of view.
  4. Only then criticise.
+ This means that your opposition will be much more receptive.
+ Can be hard (as Dennett admits himself) as some people are so ludicrous that they don't deserve the time taken.
