#+TITLE: Shred
#+FILETAGS: :linux:cli:shred:hdd:

The ~shred~ tool allows you to scrub files or partitions by repeatedly writing random noise to the target to make it
much harder to recover data.


* Whole disk

*WARNING* This is highly destructive and will wipe all data on the target, make sure you get the device right.

The following over-writes ~/dev/sda~ five times with random bits (verbosely) and makes a final write of zero's to mask
that shredding has been performed.

#+begin_example
shred -n 5 -v -z /dev/sda
#+end_example

* Alternatives

There are other alternatives such as ~wipe~ and ~scrub~.
